# Follow-up draft

Every follow-up is a draft for human review. It summarizes what was agreed in the customer's words, lists mutual next steps with an owner and a date on each side, and makes one ask. Write to `meetings/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>-followup.md`.

```markdown
DRAFT - human review required. Not sent.

To: <role at company>
Subject: <what we agreed on <date>>

<First name>,

Thanks for the time <today | on <day>>. Writing down what I heard so you can correct me.

You said the main issue is <problem in their words> and that <outcome> by <timeline> is what a good result looks like. <One sentence on the decision path if they described it.>

What we agreed:
- <Customer side>: <action> by <date> (<role>)
- <Our side>: <action> by <date> (<role>)
- <Joint>: <next meeting> on <date>

One question I did not get to: <the single highest-value evidence-gap question>.

If anything above is off, reply with the correction and I will update our notes.

<Signature>
```

## Rules

1. Their words, not ours. If the recording says "we lose two days a week to X", write that, not "your inefficiency in X".
2. Mutual steps. A follow-up with only our actions records that no customer commitment was obtained. Write that in the prep file and ask for one in the draft.
3. One ask. Not three.
4. Length under 150 words. Tables and attachments are separate.
5. No pricing, no discounts, no promises that were not made in the meeting.
6. The draft label stays on the file until a human removes it while sending.
