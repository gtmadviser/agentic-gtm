# Advance Deals

Read the opportunity, account evidence, stakeholder map, competitor notes, and relevant meeting records.

1. Summarize confirmed business problem, desired outcome, timeline, decision process, stakeholders, alternatives, commitments, and unknowns.
2. Separate customer statements from seller interpretation. Quote only supplied or accessible source material.
3. Prepare questions that close the highest-impact evidence gaps; avoid generic discovery checklists.
4. Draft a concise follow-up with mutual next steps, owner, and date. Mark it as a draft requiring human review.
5. Save redacted preparation and follow-up under `meetings/`; keep raw contacts and activity IDs in Supabase.

Do not send email, post messages, alter stages, or create tasks without a supported plan/apply workflow.
