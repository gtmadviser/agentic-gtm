# Map Market

Read `context/company.md`, `market/icp.md`, and relevant prior market artifacts.

1. Define the market question and time boundary before researching.
2. Separate direct competitors, substitutes, internal workarounds, and "do nothing."
3. For each signal record source, observed date, direct evidence, and confidence. Treat vendor claims as claims.
4. Compare competitors on buyer, job, workflow, proof, commercial model, and tradeoffs—not feature count alone.
5. Write sources and profiles under `market/`; write positioning options as hypotheses with disconfirming evidence to seek.

Do not repeat unsourced market sizes, private metrics, or customer-specific material.
