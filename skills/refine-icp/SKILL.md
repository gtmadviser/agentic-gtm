# Refine Icp

Read `context/company.md`, `market/icp.md`, recent reports, and approved evidence.

1. Maintain four separate sections: declared belief, observed evidence, exclusions/anti-ICP, and approved working definition.
2. Express fit across company, problem, trigger, stakeholder, buying constraints, and disqualifiers.
3. Attach a confidence level and evidence reference to every material criterion.
4. Challenge proxy criteria such as headcount or industry when they lack a causal link to the problem.
5. Write the revision to `market/icp.md`; log approval, owner, date, and superseded version in `strategy/decisions.md`.

Do not silently transform a hypothesis into a targeting rule.
