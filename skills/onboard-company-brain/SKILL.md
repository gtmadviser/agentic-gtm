# Onboard Company Brain

Read `OPERATING-CONTRACT.md` and existing files under `context/` and `strategy/`.

1. Ask only for facts not already present: product, target users, business model, current customers, positioning, constraints, stack, owners, and goals.
2. Label each statement as declared belief, observed evidence, hypothesis, or decision. Preserve disagreements and uncertainty.
3. Write the durable synthesis to `context/company.md`. Add material choices with owner and date to `strategy/decisions.md`.
4. End with a short missing-evidence register and the next three evidence-gathering actions.

Never put credentials, contacts, raw exports, private IDs, or unapproved customer claims in Git.
