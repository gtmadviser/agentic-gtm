# Research Accounts

Read the approved ICP, account provenance, market map, and active experiment.

1. Define allowed public sources and a freshness window.
2. For each account record factual signals with URL and observed date; label inferred pain explicitly.
3. Map the likely problem owner, users, approver, blocker, and internal alternative only where evidence supports it.
4. Score fit and urgency separately with transparent components. Missing evidence lowers confidence; it does not become a zero or a fact.
5. Keep person-level research in Supabase. Write only approved account briefs or aggregate priority rules to Git.

Never invent a trigger, quote, technology, relationship, or company event for personalization.
